# Recurring transactions

Recurring transactions are the bills, subscriptions and paychecks that repeat. FlyBudget shows
what's due, what's been paid and what's coming up, and links each payment to the transaction
that paid it.

<Screenshot name="recurring" alt="This month's recurring transactions" />

## Adding one

Choose **Add recurring** and fill in:

- **Name**, like "Rent" or "Riley's paycheck".
- **Amount** and **Type**: **Expense** or **Income**.
- **Amount Precision**: **Exact** (always the same), **Approx** (close to it, within 10%) or
  **Variable** (it changes, like an electricity bill or a pay that varies from week to week). This
  helps FlyBudget recognize the payment when it arrives. A variable amount is only an estimate, so
  a payment can be any amount when its payee or bank text recognizes the item.
- **Frequency**: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month, monthly, quarterly, twice a year or
  yearly.
- **Account**, **Start Date**, and an optional **End Date**. The amount is in the account's
  currency, so a pay into a pound account is entered in pounds.
- **Payee**, **Category** and **Notes** (optional). The payee is what FlyBudget matches
  transactions against.
- **Bank text contains** (optional): part of the text your bank shows for the payment, like
  `ACME LTD`. It isn't case-sensitive, and FlyBudget checks the bank's own text and the notes, so a
  payment still matches when the bank spells the company differently from your payee.
- **Auto-create transactions**: FlyBudget adds the transaction for you on its due date (when
  FlyBudget starts on or after that day).

<Screenshot name="recurring-add" alt="The Add Recurring dialog" dialog />

Under **Advanced**:

- **Weekend Adjustment** moves due dates that fall on a weekend to the Friday before, the Monday
  after, or the closest weekday.
- **Date Flexibility** is how many days early or late (0 to 14; 3 by default) a transaction can
  be and still count as the payment.

## Find recurring

Already have a few months of transactions? **Find recurring** looks through them for payments
to the same payee, for a similar amount, on a regular schedule (every week, every two weeks, or
on the same day of each month). Tick the ones you want and choose **Create recurring**: they're
created with their past payments already linked.

<Screenshot name="recurring-find" alt="Finding recurring transactions" />

Payees you already track aren't suggested. Transactions are only grouped when they share a
payee, so if a bank spells the same company several ways, [merge the payees](./payees#merging-payees)
first.

## Paid, upcoming and missed

When a bank sync with [Enable Banking](./bank-sync#how-syncing-works-with-enable-banking) brings in
a transaction that clearly belongs to a recurring item, FlyBudget links them and marks the payment
**Paid**. The match is clear when the transaction is in the item's account, its payee or bank text
recognizes the item, its amount fits the item's type, its date is within the item's date
flexibility, and no other payment could be it. Otherwise, and for transactions from Plaid, SimpleFIN
or a CSV file, a banner at the top of the page says a transaction may match; choose **Review now**
to accept or dismiss each match.

Each item's status:

| Status                    | Meaning                                            |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Paid**                  | A transaction paid it.                             |
| **Upcoming**              | Due soon (within your upcoming length, see below). |
| **Scheduled**             | Due later than that.                               |
| **Due**                   | Due today.                                         |
| **Missed**                | The date passed without a payment.                 |
| **Skipped**               | You skipped this date.                             |
| **Paused** / **Canceled** | The item is on hold, or no longer repeats.         |

## Pay in another currency

Say your pay comes in every week from ACME LTD in pounds, and the amount changes from one week to
the next. Add it as income with:

- **Account**: the pound account, so the amount is in pounds.
- **Amount Precision**: **Variable**, with your usual pay as the estimate.
- **Payee**: ACME LTD, and **Bank text contains** `ACME LTD` if the bank's text names the company
  differently.
- **Frequency**: weekly, and a **Date Flexibility** that covers the days the pay can arrive early or
  late.

Each week's pay is matched to its week as it arrives, whatever the amount. If the pound account is
off budget, the pay counts for your budget when you change it into your budget's currency; if it's
on budget, it counts when it arrives, converted at that day's rate. See
[Income in another currency](./accounts#income-in-another-currency).

## The Monthly tab

One month at a time, with how much income is still to come and how much of this month's bills is
paid. **List** shows income and expenses in date order; **Calendar** shows each item on its day
(click a day to jump to its rows).

<Screenshot name="recurring-calendar" alt="The calendar view" />

Each row's ⋮ menu can **Mark as paid** (adds the transaction), **Match to transaction** (links
one you already have), **Skip** this date, **Unlink transaction**, or **Edit recurring**.

## The All recurring tab

Every item you track, with its payee, account, next date, status, amount and frequency. Search
by name; canceled items are tucked away under **Show canceled recurring**.

<Screenshot name="recurring-all" alt="All recurring transactions" />

The ⋮ menu has **Mark next as paid**, **Skip next date**, **Pause** / **Resume**, **Edit**,
**Cancel recurring** (and **Restart**), and **Delete permanently**.

**Upcoming** (at the top of the tab) sets how far ahead counts as upcoming: 1 day, 1 week (the
default), 2 weeks, 1 month, the end of the current month, or your own number of days, weeks,
months or years.

## On the dashboard

The dashboard's **Upcoming Bills** card lists what's due in the next 30 days.
