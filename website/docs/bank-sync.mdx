# Connecting a bank

FlyBudget can import transactions automatically through three services. All of them are optional:
CSV import works with almost every bank and costs nothing.

|              | Plaid                                                                                  | SimpleFIN Bridge                                                | Enable Banking                                                                        |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cost**     | Free Plaid developer account                                                           | $1.50 per month, paid to SimpleFIN                              | Free for personal use (restricted mode)                                               |
| **Setup**    | Sign up for a Plaid developer account, request Production access, then enter your keys | Create an account at SimpleFIN Bridge, then paste a setup token | Register your own Enable Banking application, link your accounts, then enter its keys |
| **Best for** | People comfortable with developer dashboards                                           | Most people                                                     | Banks in Europe and the EEA                                                           |

Connect a bank from **Settings → Connected Banks**, or from the welcome screen.

## Plaid

Plaid gives you two sets of API keys:

- **Sandbox** keys work as soon as you sign up, but only with Plaid's test banks and made-up
  transactions. Use them to try FlyBudget's bank sync.
- **Production** keys connect your real banks. They aren't available at signup: you request
  Production access in the Plaid dashboard, answering a few questions about how you'll use it,
  and Plaid reviews the request. That can take anywhere from minutes to a few days.

Plaid's plans and limits for personal use change from time to time; check
[Plaid's pricing](https://plaid.com/pricing/) for what applies to you.

1. Create a free developer account at [Plaid](https://dashboard.plaid.com/signup).
2. Request **Production** access in the Plaid dashboard, and wait for Plaid to approve it.
3. Copy your **client ID** and your Production **secret**, and enter them in **Settings →
   Connected Banks** with the environment set to **Production** (or use your Sandbox secret with
   **Sandbox** to try it out first).
4. Choose **Connect Bank**. Plaid's sign-in opens in your web browser, which is how banks
   that use their own login page (like Chase) are supported.
5. Choose which FlyBudget account each bank account belongs to.

Disconnecting a bank revokes its access at Plaid before FlyBudget forgets it.

## SimpleFIN

1. Sign up at [SimpleFIN Bridge](https://bridge.simplefin.org) and connect your banks there.
2. Create a **setup token** and paste it into FlyBudget.
3. Choose which FlyBudget account each bank account belongs to.

FlyBudget exchanges the one-time token for a private access link, stored encrypted. To stop
syncing completely, disconnect in FlyBudget _and_ remove the app in SimpleFIN Bridge.

## Enable Banking

Enable Banking connects FlyBudget to banks in Europe and the rest of the EEA, under the EU's open
banking rules (PSD2). It takes a little more setup than the others: you register your own
application with Enable Banking, and then choose which of your bank accounts to import.

Enable Banking's restricted mode, which FlyBudget uses, is free for personal use. Its terms can
change, so check [enablebanking.com](https://enablebanking.com) for the current ones.

### Set up your application

1. Create an account at [Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com).
2. Register an application in the [Enable Banking control panel](https://enablebanking.com/cp/applications),
   choosing **Production**.
3. Register a **redirect URL** for the application. Enable Banking only accepts `https` addresses,
   so which one you use depends on how you run FlyBudget:
   - **Self-hosted server with HTTPS**: register the redirect URL that FlyBudget suggests under
     **Settings → Connected Banks → Enable Banking**. That's your server's own callback address,
     `https://<your-server>/api/enablebanking/callback`. Everything else is then automatic: after
     you approve at your bank, FlyBudget finishes the connection by itself.
   - **Desktop app, or a server without HTTPS**: FlyBudget can't receive the redirect, so register
     any `https` address, such as `https://localhost/flybudget-callback`, and enter the same
     address in FlyBudget.
4. Download the **private key**, a file ending in `.pem`, and keep it safe. You'll upload it to
   FlyBudget, and anyone who has it can act as your application.
5. Choose **Activate by linking accounts**. In this mode FlyBudget can only read the accounts you
   link in the control panel, so link each account you want to import there.

### Connect your bank

1. In **Settings → Connected Banks → Enable Banking**, enter the application ID, upload the private
   key and enter the redirect URL. FlyBudget checks them with Enable Banking before it saves them.
2. Choose **Connect a bank**, then pick the country and your bank.
3. Sign in and approve the connection on your bank's own page, in your web browser. Your bank
   password never passes through FlyBudget.
4. Choose which accounts to import (see [Choosing accounts](#choosing-accounts)).

:::tip[Desktop app or no HTTPS?]

After you approve, your browser lands on the redirect address you registered. The page may show an
error, and that's expected. Copy the whole address from the address bar and paste it into the
waiting screen in FlyBudget. FlyBudget takes the approval from it.

:::

### Choosing accounts

When the bank approves, FlyBudget lists the accounts it can read. For each one, choose:

- **Import as a new account**: FlyBudget creates an account with the bank's name and a suggested
  type, and imports its transactions.
- **Add to an existing account**: its transactions go into an account you already have. The two
  accounts must use the same currency.
- **Don't import it**: FlyBudget ignores the bank account. Transactions already imported stay if
  you change your mind later.

Change these at any time with **Manage accounts** on the bank's card.

An account in a different currency from your budget's starts off budget. Untick **Off budget**
right there to put it on budget, or do it later in its **Edit** dialog. See [Accounts in another currency](./accounts#accounts-in-another-currency).

Each account also has an **Import from** date, and FlyBudget doesn't import anything older than
it. For a new account it starts 90 days back. Banks often let you read further back right after
you connect, so you can pick an earlier date then. For an account you add to an existing one, it
starts the day after the newest transaction that came from a CSV file or the bank, or 90 days back
if there isn't one.

### How syncing works with Enable Banking

- **Booked transactions only.** Pending transactions are skipped until your bank books them. Their
  amounts often change when they're booked, which would otherwise create duplicates.
- **Your own entries are matched.** If you typed a transaction into that account by hand, with the
  same amount and a date within 10 days of the bank's, the bank's copy is matched to it instead of
  being imported again. Your date, payee, category and notes stay as you entered them. Reconciled
  transactions, split parts and balance adjustments are never matched. Transactions imported from
  a CSV file aren't matched either; for an existing account, the Import from date starts after
  them, so the two don't overlap.
- **Transfers are linked.** When money moves between two of your accounts in the same currency,
  FlyBudget links them as a [transfer](./transactions#transfers) if it's clear which transactions
  belong together. That's the case when the bank names the other account by its IBAN and it's one
  of yours, or when there's exactly one transaction in the other account for the same amount in the
  opposite direction, within four days. If the other side isn't imported yet, the pair is linked
  when it arrives and the match is clear. [Unlink transfer](./transactions#transfers) undoes a link.
- **Exchanges are recognized.** Money that moves between two of your accounts in different
  currencies, such as pounds out of one account and euros into another, is linked as an
  [exchange](./transactions#exchanges) when all of these are true: both accounts are imported from
  the same bank connection; one movement goes out and the other comes in, at most a day apart; the
  bank's text on each side names the other side's currency, like "Exchanged to EUR" and
  "Exchanged from GBP"; and no other
  transaction could be the other side. Anything less certain stays unlinked, and you can link it
  yourself with [Link as transfer](./transactions#linking-by-hand).
- **Recurring payments are matched.** An imported transaction is matched to a
  [recurring item](./recurring) when it clearly belongs to one: it's in the item's account, its
  payee or bank text recognizes the item, its amount fits the item's type, its date is within the
  item's date flexibility, and no other payment could be it. Anything less certain stays a
  suggestion on the Recurring page. The sync's summary counts these matches too.
- **Balances stay yours.** When FlyBudget creates an account from the bank, the first sync sets its
  starting balance so that it matches the bank's. After that, FlyBudget never changes your balances
  on its own, and a sync never changes the starting balance of an account you added to an existing
  one. Instead, the connection shows the bank's balance next to FlyBudget's, with the difference and
  a link to [reconcile](./accounts#reconciling).
- **Four automatic reads a day.** Under PSD2, banks allow four automatic reads a day when you're
  not there. FlyBudget syncs a connection at startup only if it hasn't synced in the last six hours.
  **Sync Now** always syncs. On a self-hosted server it also tells the bank you're there (it passes
  on your browser's address), which some banks need before they share data.

### Renewing access

Your consent to read a bank lasts up to 180 days, and some banks allow less. When it ends, or you
revoke it at your bank, the connection says access has ended and syncing stops. Choose
**Renew access** on its card, and approve at your bank again. Your accounts and their transactions
stay.

Some banks, Spanish ones among them, keep only one consent per user, so approving again replaces
the old one. That's why renewing updates the existing connection rather than adding a second one.
An account the bank shows for the first time is listed without an account in FlyBudget, so you can
choose what to do with it.

### Disconnecting

Disconnecting ends the bank's access at Enable Banking before FlyBudget forgets the connection. If
Enable Banking can't be reached, FlyBudget keeps the connection and asks you to try again. Your
imported accounts and transactions stay in your budget.

### Troubleshooting

- **"No accounts were shared."** Enable Banking didn't pass on any accounts. In restricted mode,
  link the accounts in Enable Banking's control panel first, then connect again.
- **"This redirect URL isn't registered in your Enable Banking application."** Add the address
  FlyBudget uses to your application's redirect URLs in the control panel, or enter one that's
  already there.
- **"Enable Banking didn't accept the application ID and private key."** Check the application
  ID, and that the `.pem` file is the private key you downloaded for that application.
- **"Access to this bank has ended."** Choose **Renew access** on the bank's card.
- **"Your bank limits how often FlyBudget can read your accounts."** Try again later.
- **"This bank only shares data when you sync it yourself."** Press **Sync Now**. If it still
  fails, the bank asks for details about your browser that FlyBudget doesn't send yet, so it can't
  be synced for now.
- **"The answer from your bank expired."** Connect again, and finish the steps promptly.
- **"Could not reach Enable Banking."** Check your internet connection (on a server, the server's),
  then try again later.

## How syncing works

- New transactions are imported when FlyBudget starts and whenever you choose **Sync Now**.
  Enable Banking connections follow their own rules, described [above](#enable-banking).
- Your [rules](./rules) run on every imported transaction.
- Reconciled transactions are never changed by a sync.
- For Plaid and SimpleFIN, account balances are adjusted to match what your bank reports.

## Your credentials

Bank tokens never leave the FlyBudget server, and they are encrypted at rest. That includes your
Enable Banking private key and bank sessions. They are never included in [backups](./backups). See
the [security overview](/community/security) for details.
